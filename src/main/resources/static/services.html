Our Services

What the Health & Wellness Center provides on Laucala Campus. Select a service to read more.

Medical Consultations

Illness and injury consultations with University Medical Officers. Book your appointment online, choosing a time and describing your reason for visit. A nurse records your vitals when you arrive, then a doctor examines you and provides treatment or advice.

  • General illness and injury assessment
  • Diagnosis and treatment guidance from University Medical Officers
  • Follow-up consultations for ongoing conditions
Book a consultation →

Preventative Care

Wound dressing and basic health screenings to catch and manage issues early, before they become bigger problems.

  • Wound cleaning and dressing changes
  • Basic health screenings (blood pressure, general check-ups)
  • Health education and preventative advice
Book a screening →

Emergency Assistance

First response and immediate treatment for injuries and urgent medical situations on campus. For life-threatening emergencies, contact campus security or emergency services immediately in addition to the Health Center.

  • First response and stabilization for on-campus injuries
  • Immediate treatment for urgent medical situations
  • Coordination with external emergency services when needed

Specialist Referrals

When a condition needs care beyond what the campus clinic can provide, University Medical Officers refer students to the National Hospital or an appropriate specialist for further diagnosis and treatment.

  • Referral letters to the National Hospital
  • Coordination with specialist care where required
  • Continuity of care via your medical record at the Health Center

Medical Report for Student Visa

Students who need a medical examination report for a student visa application can request one from the Health Center. A University Medical Officer will need to examine you before the report can be issued.

  • Valid passport or national ID
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Any existing medical records,immunization history, or prior visa medical reports
  • The visa medical examination form issued by your destination country's immigration authority if you have one
  • A list of current medications and known allergies
